Privacy notice

Effective September 20, 2026

Rising Son Coffee collects information you voluntarily provide through this website, such as your name, email address, coffee preferences, possible order quantity, consent selection, and comments.

How we use it

We use this information to manage Founding 40 reservations and feedback, send the updates you request, understand product interest and referral source, and prepare the Rising Son Coffee launch. Reserving a Founding 40 place is not a preorder, purchase or investment.

Service providers

Information submitted through the Founding 40 form may be processed by service providers used to host the website, deliver form submissions, email us, or otherwise operate the launch site. We do not intentionally sell the personal information submitted through this form.

Your choices

You can ask to stop receiving Founding 40 or launch updates, or ask us to address information you submitted, by contacting hello@therisingsoncoffee.com. We will update this notice as ordering, subscriptions, analytics, or other data practices are introduced rather than describing systems that are not yet live.

Security and minimization

We aim to collect only information useful for the feedback list and launch operations. Do not send payment-card numbers, passwords, government identifiers, or other sensitive information through the Founding 40 form.

Updates

This notice may be updated as the website, ordering system, and business operations develop. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
