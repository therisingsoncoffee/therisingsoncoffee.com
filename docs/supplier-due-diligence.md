# Supplier Due-Diligence Checklist

## Product
- Original candidate: origin/blend, roast, tasting notes, certifications/claims
- Decaf candidate: origin/blend, roast, decaffeination process, tasting notes
- available bag sizes
- whole bean / grind options
- roast-date and shelf-life practice

## Commercial
- wholesale/private-label unit price by quantity
- MOQ per SKU and per order
- sample cost/credit
- setup/artwork/plate fees
- bag, valve, label and custom-print costs
- payment terms and accepted payment methods
- price-change notice policy

## Production / fulfillment
- normal production lead time
- peak lead time
- production location
- direct-to-customer fulfillment availability and fees
- bulk shipment options
- carrier options
- tracking availability
- damaged/lost shipment handling
- returns/replacements responsibility

## Brand / compliance
- private-label rights
- required label elements
- nutrition/ingredient/allergen statements as applicable
- claim substantiation for organic/Fair Trade/etc. before publication
- barcode/UPC requirements if applicable

## Evidence standard
Record the date, supplier contact and source document/email for every commercial fact. Mark anything not explicitly confirmed as UNKNOWN.
