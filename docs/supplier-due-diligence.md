# Supplier Due-Diligence Checklist

## Product
- Original candidate: origin/blend, roast, tasting notes, certifications/claims
- Decaf candidate: origin/blend, roast, decaffeination process, tasting notes
- available net bag weights
- whole bean availability
- ground availability and grind options
- grinding surcharge, if any
- whether whole-bean and ground variants share the same MOQ
- roast-date and shelf-life practice
- sample availability/cost and whether sample cost is credited

## Commercial
- wholesale/private-label unit price by quantity
- MOQ per coffee/SKU/variant and per order
- whether Original/Decaf and bean/ground variants may be combined toward MOQ
- volume price tiers
- setup/artwork/plate fees
- bag, valve, label and custom-print costs
- grinding surcharge
- per-order or account fees
- payment terms and accepted payment methods
- price-change notice policy

## Production / fulfillment
- normal and peak production lead time
- production location
- direct-to-customer/private-label fulfillment availability
- pick/pack and fulfillment fees
- whether owner-held inventory is required
- whether supplier can fulfill one-off consumer orders
- repeat/subscription-order support
- multi-bag order handling
- bulk shipment options
- carrier options and shipping-rate basis
- tracking availability and how tracking is transmitted
- branded packing slip/inserts options and costs
- damaged/lost shipment handling
- returns/replacements responsibility
- address-correction/reship policy
- stockout/backorder handling

## Subscription compatibility
- ability to receive recurring/repeat fulfillment orders
- minimum quantity per recurring shipment
- ability to mix Original/Decaf and whole-bean/ground in one shipment
- recurring-order discounts or volume tiers
- shipping economics for 1-, 2- and 3-bag shipments
- operational handling of skips/pauses/cancellations
- any required commitment or minimum recurring volume

## Brand / compliance
- private-label rights
- required label elements
- nutrition/ingredient/allergen statements as applicable
- claim substantiation for organic/Fair Trade/etc. before publication
- barcode/UPC requirements if applicable
- lot/batch traceability available to Rising Son
- recall/quality-incident process

## Evidence standard
Record the date, supplier contact and source document/email for every commercial fact. Mark anything not explicitly confirmed as UNKNOWN. Preserve quantity tiers, effective dates and quote expiration terms. Do not publish product, certification, shipping, savings or pricing claims until the supporting evidence is retained.

## Rising Son launch constraint
Rising Son is not planning owner-home storage/packing at launch. Supplier-direct fulfillment or another professional fulfillment arrangement is therefore a launch requirement, not a convenience. Compare the complete delivered economics: coffee + packaging + label + grinding + pick/pack + shipping + processor cost + setup/order fees.
