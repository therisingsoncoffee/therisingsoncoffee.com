# Founding 40 — Launch Operations

## Purpose
Founding 40 is an early-interest community, not a preorder, donation, investment, ownership program, or obligation to purchase.

## Current customer path
1. Visitor reads the Founding 40 explanation.
2. Visitor submits the website interest form.
3. Submission is routed to hello@therisingsoncoffee.com.
4. Visitor is redirected to /thanks.html.
5. No payment is collected.
6. Product, price, shipping and fulfillment details are communicated only after supplier economics are verified.
7. Checkout remains locked until those details are approved.

## Inbox workflow
Use these statuses when handling signups:
- NEW — submission received, not yet reviewed
- CONFIRMED — valid signup/contact information
- REPLIED — personal response sent if needed
- LAUNCH READY — eligible for launch announcement
- OPTED OUT — do not send further launch updates

Never publish a claimed-spots count unless it comes from a maintained signup record.

## Rules before launch announcement
Do not insert or publish unverified:
- coffee origin or roast claims
- bag size
- retail price
- shipping cost or delivery time
- inventory quantities
- supplier identity
- refund/return promises
- launch date

## Activation test
When the public domain is reachable:
1. Submit one test through the live Founding 40 form.
2. Confirm any first-use FormSubmit activation email in the Rising Son inbox.
3. Submit a second test.
4. Verify delivery to hello@therisingsoncoffee.com forwarding destination.
5. Verify redirect to /thanks.html.
6. Verify mobile form behavior.
7. Only then describe the form as operational.
