## Mandatory qualification evidence

A supplier cannot be marked viable until the following are documented for Rising Son's exact program:

- Complete Original and Decaf product options, sensory profile, whole-bean and grind choices, bag sizes, and sample terms.
- Per-unit coffee, bag, label, packaging, setup, warehousing, pick/pack, and shipping costs/rates; MOQ; lead times; payment terms; subscriptions; integrations; replacement/refund policy.
- Written confirmation of blind direct-to-customer fulfillment and who legally manufactures, packs, and distributes.
- Facility legal name and address; applicable licenses and FDA registration status, with evidence and expiry dates where applicable.
- Food-safety plan or summary, allergen controls, sanitation controls, and flavored-coffee ingredient/allergen disclosures if flavors are offered.
- Lot/batch traceability and recall/withdrawal process with named contacts.
- Product-liability coverage and current COI with limits and expiration; ability to name Rising Son additional insured.
- Proposed indemnity and allocation of label compliance, product claims, recall, and withdrawal responsibilities.
- Final compliant label proof and supplier approval before sale.

Optional certifications or audits (SQF, BRCGS, organic, kosher, Fair Trade, etc.) must be identified as optional unless a law, contract, or chosen product claim makes one applicable. Claims require current evidence and expiry dates.
