## Joe's Garage Coffee

**Subject:** Rising Son Coffee — private-label coffee and direct-fulfillment fit

Hello,

Rising Son Coffee is evaluating roasting and fulfillment partners for a prelaunch Florida e-commerce coffee brand. Our Founding 40 program is a free reservation and product-feedback effort only: it precedes a complete verified offer and any qualifying purchase, and we are not promising order volume.

Our required operating model is supplier-direct. The supplier must source and roast the coffee; package it in compliant Rising Son-branded packaging; warehouse it; maintain batch/lot traceability; and blind-ship individual orders directly to customers. Rising Son will not roast, package, store or ship coffee from a home facility.

Please confirm whether your program can support this model and provide:

### Product and samples
- Recommended Original candidate with rich/bright aroma, low perceived acidity and bitterness, earthy tones, strong smooth full flavor and a clean aftertaste.
- A quality Decaf that stays as close as practical to the Original, including decaffeination process.
- Whole-bean and ground availability for both coffees, grind options and any surcharge.
- Available bag sizes/net weights, roast-date and shelf-life practices.
- Sample options, price, shipping cost and whether sample charges are credited. Please do not send or charge anything without written approval.

### Complete commercial and fulfillment terms
- Numeric MOQ by coffee, SKU and variant; whether Original/Decaf and bean/ground variants combine toward MOQ.
- Unit prices and volume tiers.
- Every setup, artwork, plate, label, bag, packaging, grinding, account, pick/pack and per-order fee.
- Blind direct-to-consumer fulfillment mechanics, warehousing terms, normal/peak lead times and stockout handling.
- Actual or representative shipping rates/zones for 1-, 2- and 3-bag U.S. orders, carrier options and tracking delivery.
- Shopify/API/other integration options and costs.
- Subscription/repeat-order support, cadence handling and any required recurring volume.
- Payment terms, quote validity, price-change notice and cancellation/commitment terms.
- Lost, damaged, misdelivered and quality-related replacement/return responsibilities.

### Manufacturer, food safety, label and risk evidence
- Legal names of the entities that manufacture, roast, pack, warehouse, distribute and ship the product.
- Facility name and physical location used for Rising Son orders.
- Applicable food licenses and current FDA registration status.
- Food-safety plan and allergen/cross-contact controls.
- Applicable third-party audits/certifications, clearly distinguishing mandatory requirements from optional certifications; please provide supporting documents, scope and expiration dates.
- Lot/batch traceability procedure and mock-recall/recall or market-withdrawal process, including responsible contacts.
- Product-liability coverage and a current COI showing limits and expiration; whether Rising Son can be named additional insured and at what cost.
- Proposed indemnity and allocation of label compliance, claim substantiation, recall/withdrawal, notification and expense responsibility.
- Who drafts, reviews and approves the final label, and exactly which party is legally identified as manufacturer, packer or distributor.
- For any flavored coffee, complete ingredient and allergen information plus flavor supplier documentation applicable to the selected SKU.

Please distinguish confirmed facts from items requiring quotation or later approval and attach available evidence. We are comparing candidates now and will not commit, purchase samples or authorize charges through this inquiry.

Thank you,

Rising Son Coffee
