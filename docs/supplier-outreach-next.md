Subject: Rising Son Coffee private-label Original + Decaf / direct fulfillment inquiry

Hello,

I'm building Rising Son Coffee and evaluating a small launch with two coffees: a full-flavored, smooth, low-acidity Original and a quality Decaf, ideally in whole-bean and ground variants.

We seek a professional roasting and fulfillment partner that can package under our brand and ship individual orders directly to customers in the United States, including repeat orders and possible future subscriptions. We are gathering terms and samples now; no order or volume commitment is being placed.

Could you share:

- Candidate coffees and their flavor/roast profiles, Decaf process, sample options and costs
- Available net bag sizes, whole-bean and grind options, unit prices and volume breaks, variant minimums
- Packaging, label, setup, grind, pick/pack and other per-order charges
- Shipping charges or rate method for one-, two- and three-bag orders by zone, and expected roast-to-ship time
- Blind shipping, tracking, direct-to-customer fulfillment and manual/API/Shopify integration options
- Payment terms, order cutoffs, stockout handling, damaged/lost order replacement and return policy
- Lot traceability and whether mixed Original/Decaf or bean/ground variants can share a shipment or minimum

The initial website collects free Founding 40 reservations and feedback. We will present a verified offer before taking any orders. How would your workflow handle the transition from those reservations to individual customer orders?

Thank you,

Rising Son Coffee
hello@therisingsoncoffee.com

## Follow-up procedure

1. Verify a candidate's official recipient address and direct fulfillment capability from its own site; do not infer an email from a domain.
2. Check the hosted inbox and Sent folder, Gmail forwarded inbox, and docs/supplier-outreach-tracker.csv for a prior conversation.
3. Send one tailored inquiry from the verified .com identity, record the sent date, address and message outcome.
4. Follow up once after a reasonable response interval, referring to the original thread. Avoid repeated unsolicited contacts.
5. Track a primary and at least two credible backups against the same evidence categories; do not call a backup viable until actual terms and samples/quality can be evaluated.
6. Escalate paid samples, contracts and any commitment of volume or money.
