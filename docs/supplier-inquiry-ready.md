Subject: Rising Son Coffee — private-label Original + Decaf with direct customer fulfillment

Hello,

I’m developing Rising Son Coffee, a U.S. direct-to-consumer coffee brand. We are evaluating a long-term roasting and fulfillment partner for an Original coffee with a smooth, medium-leaning profile and pleasant but strong flavor, plus a good-quality Decaf. We want you to source and roast the coffee, handle compliant Rising Son branding and packaging, hold any inventory required, and ship individual orders directly to our customers. We do not plan to store, pack, or ship coffee from our home.

Could you provide a written proposal addressing:

1. Product: recommended Original and Decaf candidates; origins/blends, roast and tasting notes, Decaf process, whole-bean and ground options, available net weights, roast-date and shelf-life practices, samples and their cost/credit terms.
2. Complete pricing: coffee, bags, valves, labels or printed bags, artwork/setup, grinding, storage, pick/pack, account/order fees, and any minimums. Please show volume tiers and whether Original/Decaf and bean/ground variants can be mixed toward a minimum.
3. Fulfillment: single-order private-label/blind shipping, one-, two-, and three-bag delivered costs to representative U.S. destinations, carriers, production and transit times, tracking, branded slips/inserts, order transmission or integration, subscription/repeat-order support, stockouts, lost/damaged shipments, and replacement/refund responsibilities. Clarify whether we must prepay or own inventory.
4. Food quality and compliance: current facility and food-safety permits/registrations as applicable, audit or certification documents available for review, lot/batch traceability, recall and complaint procedures, label approval responsibilities, allergens/cross-contact policies if relevant, and substantiation of any origin, organic, Fair Trade or other claims. Please describe your quality controls and who is responsible for a label or product defect.
5. Insurance and terms: whether you require Rising Son to carry product liability or commercial general liability coverage, the minimum limits and additional-insured requirements if any; what product/recall coverage your business carries and whether you can provide a certificate of insurance; payment terms, quote validity, price-change notice, and any contract or exclusivity requirements.

A written itemized quote and sample options would let us compare partners fairly. We are gathering information at this stage and are not placing an order or accepting terms with this request.
