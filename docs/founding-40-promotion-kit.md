## Share discipline

- Ask interested contacts once; follow up only when invited.
- Share in groups only where business announcements are allowed, with relevant context.
- Record post date, channel, URL, reach or clicks if available, reservations attributed, and comments in the outreach log.
- Do not display a remaining-spots counter until reservation eligibility and actual completed founding orders can be verified.
- Review copy when the final product, price, shipping, and benefits are known.

## Owner actions

Place the Instagram link in the profile, publish the public post on accounts you control, and send the personal message to relevant contacts. Connected social posting access has not been verified here.
