# Preliminary Economics — Known Public Inputs

These are scenario calculations, not approved retail prices. Confirm supplier pricing before use.

## Old Chicago public dropship baseline
Observed public supplier prices include US delivery, packaging, label printing, coffee bag, shipping insurance and tracking according to supplier's current public page.

| Format | Regular supplier cost | Decaf-class supplier cost |
|---|---:|---:|
| 10 oz | $12.96 | $13.21 |
| 12 oz | $16.40 | $16.65 |
| 1 lb | $19.40 | $19.53 |

Program: $10/month after trial; no MOQ; no dropship fee; manual ordering.

### Illustrative 12 oz single-bag economics before tax/refund allowance
Assuming Stripe's standard US online domestic-card fee of 2.9% + $0.30 remains applicable when activated:

At $22 retail:
- regular: revenue $22.00; supplier $16.40; processor approx $0.94; contribution approx $4.66 (21.2%)
- decaf: revenue $22.00; supplier $16.65; processor approx $0.94; contribution approx $4.41 (20.0%)

At $24 retail:
- regular contribution approx $6.60 (27.5%)
- decaf contribution approx $6.35 (26.5%)

At $25 retail:
- regular contribution approx $7.58 (30.3%)
- decaf contribution approx $7.33 (29.3%)

These figures do NOT include tax effects, refunds/replacements, promotional discounts, or the $10 monthly program cost allocation. They are useful only as a transparent benchmark against quotes.

## Decision implication
A delivered-to-customer supplier cost around $16.40–$16.65 for 12 oz can support a low-inventory launch, but a retail price near $22 leaves a relatively thin contribution margin. Better supplier economics, bundles, customer-paid shipping, or a higher price materially improve resilience.
