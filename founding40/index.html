FAQ
Does reserving make me one of the forty customers?

Not yet. Reserving gives you early access to the final offer. A completed qualifying founding order activates one of the actual forty Founding Customer positions.

Do I have to buy?

No. You will see the verified coffee, price, shipping, fulfillment and confirmed Founding 40 benefits before deciding.

Is the numbered tumbler guaranteed?

Not yet. It is our current plan. We will confirm it only after its complete cost and fulfillment are verified.

What coffee is planned?

Original and Decaf are planned for the first release. Whole-bean and ground options are being evaluated with suppliers. Final details will be shared after verification.

Is this a referral or business opportunity?

No. There is no recruiting, downline, sales requirement, commission, investment or ownership component.
