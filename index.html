FAQ
What exactly am I joining?

A forty-person email feedback list. We may send verified product options and short, optional questions before finalizing the first release.

Why only forty people?

So the first round stays manageable and every response can be read personally. It is not a rank, membership level or promise of special financial benefits.

Do I have to recruit, promote or buy anything?

No. There is no recruiting, referral requirement, fee or purchase obligation.

How will my feedback be used?

It can help compare proposed coffee options, bag sizes, price ranges and shipping choices. Rising Son makes the final decisions, and not every preference can be adopted.

When will checkout open?

Only after supplier, product, pricing, minimum order, fulfillment estimate and refund terms are confirmed.

What coffee will be available?

The planned first release is Original and Decaf. Final product details will be shared after verification.
