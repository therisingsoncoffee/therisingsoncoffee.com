The Founding 40 is our plan for the first forty Rising Son customers who help turn this idea into a real coffee company. Reserving a place is free today. Before any purchase, we will show you the verified coffee, price, shipping, fulfillment terms and complete Founding 40 offer so you can decide whether it is right for you.

Rising Son grew out of a difficult chapter and a larger goal: build a company that can eventually create meaningful support for children and families facing difficult circumstances. We are not promising an impact program that does not exist yet. The Founding 40 helps us take one real step toward building the company capable of fulfilling that mission.

  • You give: early interest, honest feedback, and—only if you like the final offer—the opportunity to become one of our first paying customers.
  • You get: first access to the verified founding offer, a voice in early decisions, and recognition as one of the customers who helped us get started.

Reserving a place is not a preorder, investment or purchase commitment. There is no recruiting, downline, sales requirement or commission. The tumbler is our current plan, not yet a guaranteed offer; it becomes a commitment only after costs and fulfillment are verified and we publish the final Founding 40 terms.

FAQ
What exactly is the Founding 40?

It is our first-customer program and early feedback group. Reserving a place is free. Reservation holders get first access to the complete verified founding offer; a qualifying founding purchase activates one of the actual forty Founding Customer positions.

What do I get out of it?

You get an early look at verified launch options, the opportunity to influence early decisions, and first opportunity to become one of Rising Son's first forty customers. Our current plan is also an exclusive individually numbered Founding 40 tumbler at no additional charge with a qualifying founding order. We will confirm that benefit before asking anyone to purchase.

Do I have to buy coffee?

No. Joining does not create a preorder or purchase commitment. If coffee later becomes available, you decide independently whether you want it.

Do I recruit people, sell coffee or earn commissions?

No. There is no downline, recruiting requirement, referral requirement, sales quota, commission or compensation plan. The Founding 40 is customer research, not a business opportunity.

Why only forty people?

Because these are intended to be Rising Son's first forty customers, not an unlimited membership tier. Keeping the group small also lets us genuinely listen to the people helping shape the launch.

How will my feedback be used?

It can help compare proposed coffee options, bag sizes, price ranges and shipping choices. Rising Son makes the final decisions, and not every preference can be adopted.

When will checkout open?

Only after supplier, product, pricing, shipping, fulfillment and refund/replacement details are confirmed.

What coffee will be available?

The planned first release is Original and Decaf. Whole-bean and ground options are being evaluated with suppliers. Final product details will be shared after verification.

Will subscriptions be available?

We are evaluating recurring delivery. We will only publish a subscription offer after the product, shipping and savings economics are verified.
